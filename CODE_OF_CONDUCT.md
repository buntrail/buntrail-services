# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming in issues, reviews and project spaces.
Respect differing experience and perspectives. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, sexualized attention and publishing private information are prohibited.
Critique the work, not the person.

Report violations privately to bungamungil@icloud.com with relevant context.
Bunga Mungil handles reports confidentially as far as practicable and may warn,
remove content or restrict participation according to severity and repetition.
Maintainers follow these standards too. Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.
